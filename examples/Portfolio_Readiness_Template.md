# Portfolio Readiness

<!-- Created as PORTFOLIO_READINESS.md at the repo root (git-ignored) during SETUP.md. A living checklist that turns the
experience docs into a portfolio that converts. Check items off as they're done. -->

## 1. Visual proof (highest leverage - text can't replace it)
A 30-60 second video or GIF beats paragraphs, especially for visual or interactive work.
- [ ] **Capture demos of current work while you still have access (with permission). Time-sensitive
      if a contract or job is ending.** List them: [...]
- [ ] Demo videos / GIFs / screenshots for personal projects: [...]
- [ ] One short reel per hero project
- [ ] Log every published video in `Project_Demo_Videos.md`

## 2. What you're allowed to show (do this BEFORE leaving an employer or client)
A portfolio of things you legally can't discuss is worthless - know the boundaries now.
- [ ] Ask each current employer/client what you may list, describe, and demo publicly; get it in writing
- [ ] Ask whether they will be a reference
- [ ] Sort each engagement into: **can show** / **can describe** / **can't mention**
- [ ] Anything not yet approved goes in `Unconfirmed_Facts.md`, not on the resume

## 3. Make the public-OK projects live, and curate GitHub
A usable live demo is worth ten described projects.
- [ ] Lead with anything a reviewer can USE in 5 seconds (live link)
- [ ] Push other public projects to live demos where feasible
- [ ] Curate your GitHub: pinned repos, READMEs with screenshots/GIFs, clear what / why / stack
- [ ] Keep private or commercial work out of public repos

## 4. Lead with a positioning narrative, not a project dump
Pick 2-3 real, defensible threads; choose the lead per target role.
- **[Thread 1]** - [role/company] -> [role/company] -> [project]. Best for [role family].
- **[Thread 2]** - ...

## 5. Write about how you work
- [ ] A short "how I work" case study (your process, tooling, what still goes wrong). Most candidates
      can't articulate their process; a concrete write-up can set you apart more than any repo.

## 6. Capture the human origin stories
Voice gold for cover letters, an about page, and interviews: why you built things, what got you
started, persistence stories.
- [Project] - [the real reason it exists]

## 7. Keep credibility anchors prominent
Don't bury them under the project list.
- [Patents, publications, awards, certifications, notable clients]
- References: [name (company)] - confirmed willing? [ ]

## 8. Job-aligned showpiece demos
Process in `SHOWPIECE_WORKFLOW.md`; ideas and gap log in `Showpiece_Backlog.md`.

## 9. Loose ends
- [ ] Confirm real start/ship dates for recent projects
- [ ] Decide the lead positioning and which 3 projects are the hero showcase
