## VERIFICATION QUESTIONS

Before finalizing any resume, verify:

1. Is every technology claim in my source document?
2. Are all metrics from documented sources?
3. Did I avoid all prohibited technologies?
4. Did I check uncertain items with user?
5. Does experience timeline match actual dates?

## RED FLAG PHRASES TO AVOID

These phrases often lead to fabrication:
- "Improved performance by X%" (unless exact and documented)
- "Reduced costs by $X" (unless documented)
- "Led team of X" (unless specified)
- "X+ years of [skill]" (verify actual dates)
- "Expert in [technology]" (verify actual proficiency)
- "Comprehensive experience with [skill]" (be honest about depth)

## WHAT TO DO WHEN JOB REQUIRES SKILLS YOU DON'T HAVE

1. **Don't add the skill** - It's better to not apply than to lie
2. **Highlight transferable skills** - Similar technologies you DO know
3. **Be honest in cover letter** - "While I haven't used X, I have extensive experience with similar Y"
4. **Ask the user** - Let them decide if it's worth applying
