# Unconfirmed Facts Registry

<!-- Created as Unconfirmed_Facts.md at the repo root (git-ignored) during SETUP.md. -->

A fact that isn't confirmed yet, or a disclosure that isn't approved, goes here instead of into the
source of truth. Nothing in **Open** may appear in outgoing material (resumes, letters, outreach,
LinkedIn, application answers). Resolve it with the named authority, update the source of truth,
then move the row to **Resolved**. Keep resolved rows as history.

When Claude finds something plausible but unverified (a metric in old notes, a "probably built",
a title it isn't sure of), it logs it here and asks, rather than using it or silently dropping it.

Authority: **User** = facts about their own work · **Employer** = disclosure approval (NDA) ·
**Lawyer** = visa, tax and legal questions
