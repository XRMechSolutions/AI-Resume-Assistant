## DISCLOSURE RISK NOTES

**Why this section exists:** Hiring involves humans with unconscious biases. Information that feels
honest and natural in conversation can trigger stereotyping or automatic filtering in written
applications and early interviews. These notes say what to include, what to avoid, and when the line
moves depending on the stage.

**General rule:** In written applications and early interviews, only disclose personal details that
directly strengthen your candidacy for THAT role. Save everything else until they're evaluating your
work rather than filtering you out.
